## Bull Oilfield Brand / Flyer Design Breakdown

### Overall Design Style

* **Industrial corporate**
* **Oilfield / instrumentation aesthetic**
* Clean layout with strong contrast
* Heavy use of:

  * dark backgrounds
  * metallic bronze accents
  * bold typography
  * sharp section divisions
* Visual hierarchy built for:

  * quick scanning
  * trade-show style marketing
  * Facebook + print flyer compatibility

---

* logo background

* background

# Layout Structure

## 1. Hero Section

* Split diagonal composition
* Left = text
* Right = shop image
* Rounded dark overlay shape

This creates:

* movement
* industrial feel
* premium corporate look

---

## 2. Service Strip

Black horizontal band with:

* icons
* short service descriptions

4-column grid:

* Rentals
* Sales
* Calibration
* Service

---

## 3. Equipment Gallery

Equal-width image grid:

* warehouse
* calibration units
* shelving
* instrumentation

Very "real operation" focused.

---

## 4. Trust Indicators

## 5. Regional Service Section

# Suggested Brand Standards

## Recommended Spacing

* 48–72 px section padding
* strong margins
* wide gutters between columns

---

## Recommended Radius

* 18–30 px rounded corners
* especially diagonal image frames

---

## Suggested Texture

Subtle:

* brushed metal
* matte grain
* carbon fiber
* soft industrial noise

Avoid:

* glossy gradients
* neon
* modern tech-glow aesthetics

---

# Best Use Cases

This style works best for:

* Facebook ads
* oilfield sales sheets
* calibration service brochures
* trade show banners
* LinkedIn company graphics
* service truck wraps
* website hero sections

# Suggested Expansion

If expanding the brand:

## Add:

* bronze metallic gradients
* blueprint overlays
* instrumentation diagrams
* pressure gauge motifs
* waveform/calibration graphics

## Avoid:

* bright colors
* minimal startup aesthetics
* AI/neon cyber themes
* excessive animations

This brand works because it feels:

## "established, dependable, industrial."

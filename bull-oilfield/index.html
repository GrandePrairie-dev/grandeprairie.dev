Equipment Sales & Rental

Chart Recorders

Mechanical and electronic circular chart recorders for pressure, temperature, differential pressure and flow measurement. Barton-compatible systems with full parts inventory. 12" circular charts, single and dual pen configurations for wellhead monitoring, production testing, and AER compliance.

Digital Dead Weights

High-accuracy digital dead weight testers for primary standard pressure calibration. Hydraulic and pneumatic configurations with electronic readout. NIST traceable for certifying transmitters, gauges, and switches up to 10,000 PSI.

Calibration Standard

Precision Gauges

Pressure test gauges, digital indicators, and wellhead monitoring gauges. Ranges from vacuum to 10,000 PSI with accuracy classes for production operations, hydrostatic testing, and instrument calibration.

All Ranges

Baker Pumps

CNC-machined Baker hand injection pumps for chemical injection, valve de-icing, and pressure testing. 10L and 20L capacities rated to 1,000, 3,000 and 10,000 PSI. Complete repair kits for all major makes and models.

Who We Are

About Bull Oilfield

Bull Oilfield Instrumentation is Grande Prairie's trusted source for oilfield measurement and testing equipment. We supply, rent, calibrate, and repair the precision instruments that Northern Alberta's producers and service companies depend on every day.

From the Montney and Duvernay plays south and west of town to the Deep Basin and beyond, our equipment is on wellheads, in test shacks, and inside plant control rooms across the Peace Region. We understand the conditions — the cold, the sour gas, the pressure — because we've been working in them alongside our customers.

Whether you need a chart recorder for a 48-hour well test, a dead weight tester to verify your shop gauges, or a Hawk digital system for permanent installation, we've got the inventory and the expertise to get you what you need, when you need it.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Quick answers about rentals, calibration, service area, and emergency support.

Do you rent chart recorders in Grande Prairie?
Yes. We rent mechanical and electronic chart recorders for wellhead monitoring, production testing and AER-compliant data logging. Same-day dispatch is typically available throughout the Peace Region. Call (780) 532-5959 to confirm fleet availability.
Are your calibrations NIST traceable?
Every pressure and temperature calibration we perform is referenced to NIST primary standards using digital dead-weight testers, and we issue documented certificates suitable for AER compliance audits.
What pressure range can you calibrate and test?
Our digital dead-weight testers and gauges cover vacuum through 10,000 PSI. Field rental gauges and Hawk recorders are available across the full oilfield service range from 1,000 PSI up to 15,000 PSI.
Do you service Calscan Hawk digital recorders?
Yes — we sell, rent, install, and calibrate Calscan Hawk 9000 surface data loggers, including battery-powered Class 1 Div 1 rated units suitable for −40°C operation with internet streaming.
What is your service area?
Grande Prairie and the surrounding Peace Region, all of Northwest Alberta (NWAB — Montney, Duvernay, Deep Basin), and Northeast British Columbia. Field service is available to wellsites and facilities throughout the WCSB.
Are you available after hours?
Yes — on-call before and after business hours by calling (780) 532-5959. Standard business hours are Monday–Friday, 7 AM – 4 PM. Same-day rental dispatch is typical during business hours.
Do you rebuild Barton chart recorders?
Yes — Barton recorder rebuilds are a core specialty. We stock chart paper, pens, pen arms, chart drives, clock mechanisms, pressure elements and diaphragms in Grande Prairie for fast turnaround.

What “best kind” means

On the Northeast Avalon — and in kitchens all over NL — best kind is the highest compliment: not fancy, just right. That’s the spirit here. Salt air and fog don’t reach Alberta, but the baking tradition did.

Home in Grande Prairie

We’re part of the Newfoundland diaspora out west — rig hands, nurses, teachers, trades, whole families — who landed in the Peace Country and still miss a proper bun. Everything is baked in our prepared home kitchen in Grande Prairie; you pick up local, same as the logo says.

Made with love — for real

Allergies, a big crowd, a tray for the fire hall — send a message and we’ll do our best to sort you out. If we can’t safely accommodate something, we’ll tell you straight.

Ready when you are.

Bake weeks and pickup slots are posted on our Facebook page — send a message there to hold bread, rolls or a sweet pan. We’ll confirm details and pickup time in Grande Prairie.
